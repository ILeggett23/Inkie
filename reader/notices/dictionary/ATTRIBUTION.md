Inkbound uses a derived, bounded binary index containing 151,384 entries,
including WordNet exception-form mappings. Original glosses (including any
quoted examples) and supplied parts of speech are retained. Pronunciation is
not supplied by WordNet and is not invented. This is a substantial lexical
dataset, not complete English coverage. Unknown entries return not found.
